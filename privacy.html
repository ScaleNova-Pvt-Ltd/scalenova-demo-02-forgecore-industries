Data Governance

Confidentiality & IP Protection Policy

At ForgeCore Industries, we recognize that engineering blueprints, CAD solid models, and component specifications constitute core intellectual property. We treat all submitted documents under strict industrial non-disclosure agreements (NDAs).

1. Engineering CAD Model Security

All STEP, IGES, DXF, and PDF models transmitted through this portal are encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3 and stored on air-gapped engineering storage arrays accessible only to authorized DFM staff.

2. Non-Disclosure Commitment

ForgeCore executes standard mutual NDAs prior to the initiation of any physical die tooling or prototype production. We never share customer designs, drawings, or proprietary alloy treatments with third parties.
