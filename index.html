01 // FORGING

Closed & Open-Die Heavy Forging

High-integrity structural forgings up to 12 metric tons. Controlled grain flow alignment for crankshafts, connecting rods, and transmission bevel gears.

02 // MACHINING

5-Axis High-Velocity CNC Milling

Continuous 5-axis DMG MORI machining centers. High-tolerance contoured aero-structures, turbine impellers, and complex fluid manifold housings.

03 // METALLURGY

Superalloy & Titanium Machining

Specialized cryogenic tooling for Grade 5 Titanium (Ti-6Al-4V) and Inconel 625/718 alloys engineered for aerospace propulsion and downhole energy tools.

04 // HEAT TREATMENT

Automated Atmosphere Quench & Temper

In-house computer-controlled continuous carburizing, induction surface hardening, and gas nitriding to achieve exact Rockwell C specifications.

05 // QUALITY ASSURANCE

Zeiss Coordinate Metrology & NDT

Laser scanning CMM verification, magnetic particle inspection (MPI), and ultrasonic flaw detection with 100% material traceability certs (EN 10204 3.1).
