# Live Client Demo Script — ForgeCore Industries

Use this walkthrough when presenting ScaleNova's manufacturing capability to industrial SME owners:

1. **The First 3 Seconds (Visual Credibility)**:
   - Open `index.html`.
   - Highlight the heavy industrial design aesthetic: Deep Graphite, Amber accent lines, and the dynamic interactive isometric blueprint wireframe canvas.
   - Point out that there is zero generic AI imagery — the UI looks like an established, audited precision supplier.

2. **Manufacturing & Technical Depth**:
   - Navigate through `capabilities.html` and `manufacturing.html`.
   - Show the 10,000-Ton press parameters, Mazak 5-axis envelopes, and metallurgy standards (DIN 1.2714, Inconel 718).

3. **High-Converting Engineering RFQ Flow**:
   - Open `request-quote.html`.
   - Walk through the structured RFQ fields: Material Grade, Annual Volume, Tolerance Specifications, and Cloud CAD Drawing Links.
   - Submit a live quote. Show the generated `SN-FOR-XXXXXX` confirmation slip.
   - Switch to the master Google Sheet (`Demo2_Manufacturing`) or Frappe CRM to show that the engineering team has instantly received the lead.
