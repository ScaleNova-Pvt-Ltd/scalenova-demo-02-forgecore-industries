SERIES FORGING PROTOCOL

The 5-Phase Component Lifecycle

Walk through the automated workflow that converts certified raw ingot billets into finished AS9100 aerospace components.

Phase 01 • Billet Preparation

Precision Induction Core Heating

Ingot billets are cut to weight with cold-circular carbide saws, weighed automatically, and brought to forging temperature in computer-controlled induction coils to prevent scaling.

✓ 100% Optical Pyrometer In-Line Check
Phase 02 • Hot Deformation

Preform & Finish Closed-Die Strike

The 10,000T hydraulic press executes multi-stage strikes with automated robotic die lubrication. Unbroken metallic grain flow aligns dynamically along mechanical load paths.

✓ Microstructure Grain Alignment Assured
Phase 03 • NADCAP Heat Treatment

Solution Annealing, Quenching & Aging

Parts enter automated atmosphere-controlled furnaces followed by rapid mechanical transfer into agitated polymer or water quench tanks in under 12 seconds.

✓ AMS 2750G Pyrometry Certified
Phase 04 • Finish Dimensions

5-Axis High-Speed Finish Contouring

DMG Mori 5-axis machining centers execute finish cuts with high-pressure 70-bar coolant, achieving Ra 0.4 surface finishes and ±0.005 mm profile tolerances.

✓ In-Process Laser Tool Calibration
Phase 05 • Quality Assurance

CMM Metrology & Ultrasonic NDT Inspection

Every component undergoes immersion ultrasonic scanning (MIL-STD-2154 Class A), magnetic particle or fluorescent penetrant inspection, and Zeiss coordinate measuring.

✓ Full Material Test Report (MTR) Sign-off
