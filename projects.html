Challenge: 120-piece delivery required within 10 weeks, zero detectable internal grain voids under AMS 2631 Class AA ultrasonic testing.

CASE STUDY #02

Heavy Commercial Vehicle Planetary Ring Gears

Client: Leading Heavy Commercial EV Manufacturer
Material: 20MnCr5 Forged Case-Hardening Alloy
Challenge: Severe noise-vibration-harshness (NVH) failure in cast competitors under high electric torque peaks.

Outcome: Transitioned customer to closed-die continuous-grain forging, boosting torsional fatigue threshold by 42% while reducing unit raw material cost by 18%.
