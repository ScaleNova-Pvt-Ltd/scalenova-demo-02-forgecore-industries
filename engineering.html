STAGE-GATE QUALITY GATES

From 3D CAD Ingestion to AS9102 FAI Sign-off

Review the four milestone quality gates required before serial production begins.

Gate 01 • 24-48 Hours

Geometric & Draft Angle Feasibility Review

Direct ingestion of native Catia, SolidWorks, or STEP CAD models. Checking draft angles (3° to 7°), corner fillets, parting line symmetry, and machining envelope margins.

✓ Automated DFM Feasibility Dossier Provided
Gate 02 • Days 3 to 7

FEA Grain-Flow & Flash Volume Optimization

Thermomechanical virtual forging simulating press loads, die contact temperatures, and effective strain rates. Adjusting preform roll dies to guarantee flaw-free cavity fill.

✓ 100% Virtual Tryout Sign-off

High-Speed Hard Milling of Impression Dies

Machining premium AISI H13 die blocks in our climate-controlled tool room. Cavities are hand-polished to mirror Ra 0.2 finish to ensure flawless hot metal release.
