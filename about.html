Plant Heritage & Heavy Infrastructure

30 Years of Uncompromising Metallurgical Precision

Operating out of 3 modern manufacturing plants spanning 45,000 m² under crane, ForgeCore supplies mission-critical structural forgings and precision machined assemblies to Fortune 500 engineering corporations.

Heavy Plant Capital Assets

Our Pune plant (Works 1) houses 4 closed-die forging lines anchored by a 10,000-ton hydraulic forging press, induction billet heaters, and automated manipulators.

Works 2 in Coimbatore is dedicated to climate-controlled multi-axis machining with 45 horizontal and vertical CNC machining centers, while Works 3 in Chennai serves as our export staging, ultrasonic immersion testing, and packaging logistics terminal.

Works 1 Specifications
  • Press Tonnage: 1,200T to 10,000T Closed Die
  • Hammer Fleet: 3T, 5T, 8T Counterblow Systems
  • Billet Cutting: Kasto High-Precision Bandsaws
  • Heat Treat: Continuous Quench & Temper Line
